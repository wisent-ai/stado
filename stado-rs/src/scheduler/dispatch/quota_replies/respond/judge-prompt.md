You read the latest message Microsoft support wrote on an Azure GPU quota increase ticket, so an automated responder can decide what to send back.

Decide whether the message says the request is held by a billing or payment problem on the customer's side — a payment history, a declined card or bank payment, an outstanding balance, an unpaid invoice, anything the customer must settle before quota can be granted — or whether it asks for information about the request (region, deployment model, VM families, cores) or says something else.

The message, in any language:
{message}

Choose BILLING when the message says the request is held by such a billing or payment problem, naming the problem in the reason; choose OTHER for anything else, saying in the reason what the message asks or says.
